# Spring tide

The higher tide that follows a new or full moon, when sun and moon pull together. In
[[Silverstadt]] it is the tide that puts water in the Tidewater cellars twice a month, and the
reason the double tide bell exists at all.

Not named for the season. A neap tide is the opposite: the weakest tide, a week either side of
a half moon.
