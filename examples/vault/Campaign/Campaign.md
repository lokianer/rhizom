# Silverstadt

A city campaign. Silverstadt sits where the river Selle meets a tidal estuary, and everything in it is about tides, bells and records: who owns what, who owes what, who wrote it down and where the writing went. The party arrived in [[Silverstadt]] looking for work on the docks and has, over thirteen sessions, become mixed up with all three of the city's factions without joining any of them.

## The party

- **Tessaly Vane**, human fighter (battle master), from the northern hill towns. Carries the map.
- **Orrin Quill**, halfling bard (lore), the party's face and the reason they know [[Mira Voss]].
- **Dagny**, goliath barbarian, hates boats, currently owns one.
- **Ilex**, wood elf ranger (gloom stalker), quiet, keeps the ledger of who owes the party favours.

Rules of the table: no phones except for character sheets, initiative is rolled once per combat, and anyone can call a ten-minute break.

## Current state (after session 13)

The party holds the tidewater charts recovered in [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive]] and has not yet decided whether to hand them to [[Corvin Marsh]] or sell them through [[Sable]]. [[Aldric Thane]] suspects them of the fire at the Municipal [[Campaign/Places/Archive|Archive]]. See [[Session 13 – Ashes and Ink]] for the mess.

## Themes I keep coming back to

Tides as a metaphor for information: what is written down floods and recedes, and what survives is whatever was kept on high ground. This is also, not coincidentally, what the research notes are about; see [[Rhizomes]] for the root system and [[Mycorrhizal networks]] for the one that actually trades.
