# Milieus of Silverstadt

Where the people of the campaign stand, drawn the way the chart in [[Sinus-Milieus]] is drawn:
two axes nobody measured, and the names placed between them by eye.

Left to right runs `outlook`, from those who want the city kept as it is to those who would
gladly see it turned over. Bottom to top runs `standing`, from the cellars under Lantern Quay to
the council chamber. Each is a number from 0 to 100 in the character's own note, so somebody who
moves during play moves here by having their frontmatter edited, and this page is never a second
place where the truth is kept.

Two of them stand nowhere yet. [[Brannoc Ironweld]] has an outlook and no standing — he sells to
all three factions and is owed by all three, and I have not worked out what that is worth.
Nobody at the table has worked out what [[Elder Wren]] wants at all.
