# Archive

The Municipal Archive on Bell Square, built after the flood of 841 to replace the [[Sunken Archive]]. Three storeys of records, a reading room, and since [[Session 13 – Ashes and Ink]] a burnt-out east wing.

Not to be confused with [[Research/Archive]], which is where research fragments go to wait.

## Before the fire

Public records: deeds, debts, births, tide tables. The [[The Archivists|Archivists]] ran it and charged for lookups by the hour; [[Corvin Marsh]] has an office on the second floor. Anyone could go in. Almost nobody did.

## The fire

Started in the east wing on the night after session 12. Deed room and debt registers destroyed; the tide tables survived because they were on loan to Brannoc. [[Aldric Thane]] believes it was arson aimed at one record. [[Mira's Ledger|Mira Voss]] has said nothing, which is itself information.
