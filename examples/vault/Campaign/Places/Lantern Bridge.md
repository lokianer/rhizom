# Lantern Bridge

The only bridge. Seven stone arches, a watch house at the north end where [[Aldric Thane]] keeps his ledger, and a row of lanterns that the [[Order of the Lantern]] lights at dusk and, the party has now noticed, sometimes at other times too.

## The Cracked Bell

Tavern on the bridge's third pier, built out over the water on timber that should not still be standing. The party's base. The landlady, Hesta, extends credit against loot and has never been wrong about a piece's value.

## What happens here

- Ferries tie up beneath the arches when the tide bell rings
- The bridge bell rings when something is wrong on the river; twice this campaign, both times before something the party did
- [[Session 11 – Under the Lantern Bridge]] took place almost entirely on and under it

## Under the bridge

The second arch from the south hides a culvert into the Undercroft. Sable uses it. According to the tidewater charts (GM only) it also leads to the [[Sunken Archive]]'s vault level, which nobody has told the party.
