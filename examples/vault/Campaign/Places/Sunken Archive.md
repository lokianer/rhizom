# Sunken Archive

The original archive of Silverstadt, drowned in 841 when the river changed course during a storm tide. The Archivists rebuilt above ground on Bell Square (see [[Archive]] for that building) and spent the next two centuries pretending the lower one did not exist, then the century after that quietly trying to get back in.

> [!note] Naming
> In the notes and at the table this is "the Sunken Archive"; the Archivists call it "the lower stacks"; Wren calls it "the Drowned Library"; Sable calls it "downstairs". They mean the same place.

## Layout

Three levels, only the first mapped.

1. **Upper gallery.** Kept dry by [[Brannoc Ironweld]]'s pump. Reading desks, empty shelves, the Archivists' working camp. Entered from the warehouse cellar.
2. **The stacks.** Waist-deep at low tide, flooded at high. Shelves are stone, so some records survived in sealed jars. The party recovered the tidewater charts from here in [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive]].
3. **The vault.** Not reached. Below the stacks, behind a door the water has not opened in a generation. [[Corvin Marsh]] thinks the charts say when it can be reached.

## Hazards

- Tide: the stacks fill in under an hour once the tide bell rings
- The pump fails if nobody tends it; two hours of grace
- Ink eels. Not dangerous, but they ruin paper and Orrin is frightened of them

## Things found here

Loot is tracked per session; see [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive#Loot]]. The charts themselves are described in [[Recover the Tidewater Charts]].
