# Silverstadt

A city of about forty thousand on the estuary of the Selle, named for the colour of the water at low tide over the mud flats rather than for any metal. Bells run everything: the tide bells on the quays, the hour bells on the Bellward tower, the warning bell on the [[Lantern Bridge]]. A Silverstadter can tell the time and the state of the tide with their eyes closed and will tell you so.

![map](assets/silverstadt-map.svg)

## Districts

Brannoc's sketch, cleaned up. North is up; the river runs west to east and the tide comes in from the east.

### The Bellward

The high ground, the old town. The hour tower, Bell Square with [[Mira's Ledger|Mira Voss]]'s house, the Municipal [[Campaign/Places/Archive|Archive]] (what is left of it) and the chapter house of the [[Order of the Lantern]].

### Tidewater

Piers, warehouses, pumps. Floods at spring tides and nobody minds. [[Brannoc Ironweld]] works from the third pier. The [[Sunken Archive]] is under the eastern end, reached through a warehouse cellar the [[The Archivists|Archivists]] own.

### Lantern Quay

The south bank, poorer, livelier. Reed beds downstream where [[Elder Wren]] lives. [[The Gutter Court]] runs the quay's back streets and pretends it does not.

### The Undercroft

Not a district on any official map: the culverts, cisterns and cellars that connect everything else. Sable's territory. See [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive#Getting in]] for the party's only visit so far.

## History in three lines

Founded as a ferry crossing in 612 (the city counts years from the founding of the first chapter house, which is confusing and which I have stopped fighting). Grew on the salt and record trades. The Archive drowned in 841, was rebuilt above ground, and the drowned part was never fully recovered, which is the whole campaign.
