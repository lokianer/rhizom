# The Gutter Court

The thieves of Lantern Quay and the Undercroft, organised loosely enough that they can deny being organised at all. "Court" because disputes are settled by a sitting of the senior members in a drained cistern; the party has seen it once, from a distance.

## Structure

- The **Bench**: five seniors, names not all known
- The **Purse**: [[Mira Voss]], though she would deny it in public
- Runners, fences, night porters: [[Sable]] is the one the party deals with

## Goals

Money, obviously, but also the tidewater charts, for reasons that come from [[Mira's Ledger]] and not from the Bench. The Bench thinks the charts are a smuggling route. Mira knows better.

## Attitude to the party

Wary. The party has been useful twice and inconvenient once. [[A Debt to Mira]] is the lever.

## Territory

Lantern Quay back streets, the Undercroft, the second arch of the [[Lantern Bridge]]. Not the Bellward; the Order sees to that.
