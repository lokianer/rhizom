# Order of the Lantern

Paladins and lay members who keep the [[Lantern Bridge]] and, by a charter nobody has read for a century, the river between the tide marks. Uniform: dark blue coat, lantern badge. Motto, over the watch house door: *We keep the crossing*.

## Goals

Public: order on the river. Private: the Order's founding charter is in the [[Sunken Archive]] and they would like it back before the [[The Archivists|Archivists]] read it, because the Order suspects it says something about the lanterns they would rather it did not.

## Members the party knows

- [[Aldric Thane]], captain of the night watch
- Commander Ferrant, met once, formidable
- Sergeant Pell, who lost the watch lantern the party returned in session 7

## Secret (GM only)

The lanterns on the bridge are not lit for light. They are lit in a pattern that keeps something under the river asleep. The Order has forgotten why and kept doing it.
