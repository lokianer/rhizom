# The Archivists

Keepers of Silverstadt's records, above and below the water. Thirty members, most of them clerks; three Keepers. They own the Municipal [[Campaign/Places/Archive|Archive]] on Bell Square and the warehouse cellar that leads to the [[Sunken Archive]].

## Goals

To recover the lower stacks and the vault beneath them. [[Corvin Marsh]], the Second Keeper, is running that effort and hired the party for it. The First Keeper, Ondine Vale, has not met the party and is said to disapprove of adventurers on principle.

## Methods

Patience, pumps and payment. The Archivists do not fight; they wait, and they keep records of who wronged them. Their oath is "written, kept, found".

## Relations

- [[Order of the Lantern]]: mutual suspicion dressed as courtesy
- [[The Gutter Court]]: they buy stolen records back from Sable without asking where she got them
- [[Mira's Ledger|Mira Voss]]: the Archive owes her money, and no Keeper will explain why

## Loose end

Marsh's predecessor as Second Keeper, a woman called Anwen Thistle, retired to the reed beds ten years ago. Wren knew her. Nobody has connected these facts yet, including me until I wrote this sentence.
