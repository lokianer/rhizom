# A Debt to Mira

Orrin borrowed three hundred gold from [[Mira Voss]] in session 8 to buy Dagny's boat out of impound. He did not read the terms. The terms, as the party learned in [[Session 11 – Under the Lantern Bridge]], include a clause that the debt may be called in "in kind", and Mira has now hinted what kind: the tidewater charts.

## Tasks

- [x] Discover what Orrin actually signed
- [x] Fail to renegotiate (Orrin rolled a 3)
- [ ] Pay 300 gp before the due date, or
- [ ] Trade the charts, or
- [ ] Find out what the ledger says the Archive owes her and use it
- [ ] Get Ilex to stop bringing it up

## Mira's position

She does not want the money. She wants the charts, or failing that, the party in her ledger for good. See [[Mira's Ledger#The ledger]] for what the party has reconstructed.

## Ways out I have prepared

1. The party sells something from [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive#Loot]]; the astrolabe alone would cover it
2. Sable arranges a theft of the relevant page; the Cat wants out, see [[Sable]]
3. Wren reveals that Mira's own name is in the Archive's records as a debtor, from before the ledger

Whatever they choose, the due date lands right before the vault's low cycle. That is deliberate.
