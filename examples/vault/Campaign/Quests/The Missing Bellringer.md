# The Missing Bellringer

Harl Bellringer, who rang the hour bells on the Bellward tower for thirty years, did not ring the dawn bell on the day after session 11. The city noticed within a minute. Hesta, who was fond of him, asked the party to find out what happened, quietly.

## What the party knows

- Harl owed [[Mira Voss]] forty gold; she struck the debt through the same day
- He was last seen leaving the Municipal [[Campaign/Places/Archive|Archive]] with a bundle
- [[Elder Wren]] said "bells carry further over water" and would not elaborate
- The bridge bell rang that night; nobody rang it

- [ ] Ask [[Aldric Thane]] whether the Order logged the bridge bell
- [ ] Find the bundle

## Leads

Sable knows something and is frightened; see [[Sable#Hooks]]. The tide tables from Harl's room are the same series Brannoc borrowed from the Archive, which means somebody wanted a complete set.

## Resolution

Not yet. My plan: Harl is alive and in the Undercroft, hiding from whoever set the fire, ringing the bridge bell to tell someone he is alive. The party will hear the pattern and Wren can read it.
