# Session 13 – Ashes and Ink

Three players; Ilex's player was away, so Ilex "went to check on the boat" all session. Opened with the fire, as planned.

## The fire

The party woke at The Cracked Bell to the bridge bell and smoke over the Bellward. East wing of the Municipal [[Campaign/Places/Archive|Archive]] gone. [[Captain Thane]] found them in the crowd and was, for the first time, cold with them: the party was last seen leaving the Archivists' cellar the evening before with sealed jars. He did not accuse. He wrote their names in his pocket ledger while they watched.

## Ink

[[Corvin Marsh]] in the ruined reading room, sorting wet paper by hand. He asked for the charts again, more quietly. Orrin asked what the Archive owed Mira Voss. Marsh said, "Everything, once," and would not go on. Good line; I did not plan it.

Among the burnt records Tessaly found a scorched catalogue card referring to a bound volume the Archive had held on deposit: [[The Ashen Codex]]. No such book exists in any note of mine yet, which means the players have handed me a plot hook and I intend to use it.

## Ashes

Brannoc's apprentice is confirmed missing. Brannoc has stopped talking to his machines. Dagny promised to find the boy, which means the party now has three quests and a debt.

## Lady Vermillion

A name the party overheard from two Order members near the ruin: "[[Lady Vermillion]] will want to know." Nobody in my notes has that name. Sable, when asked, went very still and said she did not know anyone by it, which was the first large lie the party has caught her in.

## Ending

Party split on what to do with the charts: Orrin wants to read them with Wren, Dagny wants to sell, Tessaly wants to hand them over and be done. Ended on that argument, which is the best kind of ending. Next session on the 24th.
