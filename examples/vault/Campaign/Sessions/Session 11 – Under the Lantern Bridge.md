# Session 11 – Under the Lantern Bridge

Recap read at the table: the party had just found the [[Sunken Archive]]'s side door with Sable's help and were resting at The Cracked Bell on the [[Lantern Bridge]] when Mira Voss sent a note.

## The note

Mira's note called in Orrin's debt "in kind" and named the tidewater charts, which the party had not yet heard of. Cue an evening of asking everyone what the charts were. [[Aldric Thane]] warned them off [[The Gutter Court]] in the watch house and used "the river remembers" twice.

## The boat

Dagny's boat, tied under the second arch, had been searched. Sable turned up sitting in it, eating, and admitted she had done the searching on the Bench's orders and found nothing, and said she would say so to the Bench. Dagny decided this counted as a favour owed to *her*. Sable agreed, which is how the [[Sable#Hooks]] entry came about.

## Under the bridge

Ilex went into the culvert behind the second arch. Perception check, natural 20: a bell mechanism in the wall of the culvert, rope running downwards into the dark. This is the bridge bell's second rope. Nobody knew there was one. Ilex left it alone. Good.

## Ending

The party agreed to take [[Corvin Marsh]]'s job so that they would at least know what the charts were before anyone took them. Session ended with the tide bell and a plan to go down at the next low tide.

## Loot

None. Hesta extended another week of credit.
