# Session 12 – The Sunken Archive

The dungeon session. Went well; the tide as a timer worked exactly as intended and I will use it again.

## Getting in

Through the [[The Archivists|Archivists]]' warehouse cellar, then the upper gallery. The pump was failing; [[Brannoc Ironweld]]'s valve fixed it after a tinker's tools check from Tessaly (she has them because of a backstory detail from session 2 that finally mattered). Brannoc's apprentice was mentioned as "at the Archive today"; nobody registered it. They will.

## The stacks

Waist-deep, cold, the [[Drowned Library]] proper. Stone shelves, sealed jars, drowned ledgers gone to pulp. Ink eels, played for comedy, worked. Row 7, bell shelf, three jars. Forty minutes of table time for what the charts said was an hour of tide.

> [!note] Ruling made at the table
> Stealth in waist-deep water is at disadvantage unless you stop moving. Keeping this.

## The bell

At the far end of the stacks, a bell hanging above the water with no rope. It rang once as the party left. Nobody touched it. Orrin's player has since asked three times about it. Connects to the neap-tide rumour in [[Silverstadt#Rumours the party has heard]].

## Ending

Out with the tide bell ringing. Marsh met them in the gallery, paid the first half, asked for the jars, and the party said "tomorrow". He did not like it. The fire at the Municipal Archive happened that night; the players do not know yet.
