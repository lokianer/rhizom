# Brannoc Ironweld

Runs a repair shop on the third pier of Tidewater, mostly pumps and winches. Built the bilge pump that keeps the [[Sunken Archive]]'s upper gallery dry enough to walk in, which is how the party met him: [[Corvin Marsh]] sent them to fetch a replacement valve in [[Session 12 – The Sunken Archive]].

#campaign/silverstadt/npcs

## Manner

Talks to machines while he works and to people as if they were machines that had come in with a fault. Good-natured about it. Owes [[Mira's Ledger|Mira Voss]] money like half of Tidewater does and does not think it matters.

## Useful for

- Repairs and identification of mechanical loot
- Knows every drain and culvert under Tidewater; drew the party the sketch that became [[Silverstadt#Districts]]
- Will build a custom item for cost plus a story he has not heard before
