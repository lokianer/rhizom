# Corvin Marsh

Second Keeper of [[The Archivists]], which makes him responsible for the [[Sunken Archive]] and, since the fire, for what is left of the Municipal [[Campaign/Places/Archive|Archive]] too. Thin, ink-stained, wears reading lenses on a cord and forgets they are there.

#campaign/silverstadt/npcs

## What he wants

The tidewater charts back in Archivist hands, and the party not to read them. He has not explained why. He pays well and promptly, which the party finds more suspicious than if he haggled.

## What he knows (GM only)

The charts show that the lower Sunken Archive floods on a cycle, and that the next low cycle is in about six weeks. Something is stored down there that the Archivists have not been able to reach for a generation. He does not know what; his predecessor did and did not write it down, which he regards as the worst thing a person can do.

> [!warning] Do not reveal yet
> The charts also show a second entrance under the [[Lantern Bridge]]. Keep this for when the party goes back.

## Relationship

Employer, slightly warming. He has started leaving books out where Orrin will see them.

## Stats

Mage stat block, but swap *fireball* for *dispel magic*; he would never use fire near paper.
