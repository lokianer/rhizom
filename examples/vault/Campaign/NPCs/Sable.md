# Sable

Fence, rooftop courier and the only member of [[The Gutter Court]] the party actually likes. Lilac skin, filed horns, a coat with more pockets than seams. Calls herself a "night porter". Everyone else calls her the Cat, a name [[Elder Wren]] used first.

#campaign/silverstadt/npcs

## Relationship to the party

Rivalry with Ilex that both are enjoying. She sold the party the location of the [[Sunken Archive]]'s side door in session 10 and has offered to move the tidewater charts for a third of the price. She has not mentioned that [[Mira's Ledger|Mira Voss]] would take the other two thirds.

## Manner

Never uses the front door of anything. Eats constantly. Lies about small things to keep in practice and is scrupulously honest about large ones, which the party has not worked out yet.

- [ ] Knows who set the Archive fire and is frightened of them, which is new
