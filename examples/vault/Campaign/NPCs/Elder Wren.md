# Elder Wren

Keeps the reed beds below Lantern Quay and has for longer than anyone on the quay has been alive, which she does not explain. Small, sun-browned, barefoot in all weather. The river's memory in a person, if [[Aldric Thane]] is the city's.

#campaign/silverstadt/npcs

## Role in the campaign

The party met her in session 9 when Dagny's boat ran aground in the reeds. She knows the estuary's tide tables by heart and could read the tidewater charts on sight, which none of the factions have realised. She is the party's way of learning what the charts mean without asking [[Corvin Marsh]].

## Manner

Answers questions with a question about the weather. Feeds the herons. Refuses coin and accepts stories, songs and repairs to her landing stage, which is why she likes [[Brannoc Ironweld]].
